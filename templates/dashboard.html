Academic Viva Note for Evaluator:

Why Random Forest outperforms Linear Regression and Decision Tree: A single Decision Tree suffers from high variance (overfitting to local sample splits). Random Forest utilizes Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregation) by fitting 100 decorrelated trees with random feature subsampling. Averaging the ensemble predictions drastically lowers the estimator variance, achieving an R² score of 99.01% vs 94.62% for Linear Regression and 98.15% for a single Decision Tree.
